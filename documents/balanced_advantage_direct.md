# HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund
## Minimum SIP
The minimum SIP and lump-sum application amount shown is ₹100.
## Exit load
Up to 15% of units from each purchase/switch-in may be redeemed without exit load. Redemption above that limit is subject to 1.00% exit load within 1 year; no exit load applies after 1 year.
## Riskometer
The current riskometer shown on the scheme page is Very High Risk.
## Benchmark
The benchmark is the NIFTY 50 Hybrid Composite Debt 50:50 Index.
## Objective
The fund aims to provide long-term capital appreciation/income from a mix of equity and debt investments.
